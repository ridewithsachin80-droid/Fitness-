# FitLife — real-phone checks (record sheet)

These five need a person holding an Android phone; no robot can do them. They must all pass before Sachin is asked for production approval.

**Before you start**
- Test site only: fitness-test.up.railway.app.
- Use a test-site member login. Never write logins, PINs or OTPs on this sheet or in screenshots.
- Do every check once in **dark** and once in **light** (Settings → Appearance).
- Screenshot each result.

| # | Check | How | Passes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Microphone** | Today → Speak → allow the mic → say "two idli and sambar for breakfast" → Save. | The phone asks for the mic once; the words appear; the review card shows idli and sambar; after Save, Today's total goes up. |
| 2 | **Camera** | Today → Snap → allow the camera → photograph a plate (or a food pack) → review → Save. | The phone asks for the camera once; the photo is taken in the app; foods are suggested; after Save, Today's total goes up. |
| 3 | **Keyboard** | Open Weight, then Food → "+ Add food item", then the AI box ("Log or ask FitLife"), and type in each. | The keyboard never covers the box you are typing in or its Save/Done button; the screen does not jump sideways; the number keypad opens for weight. |
| 4 | **Installing the app** | Chrome → ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen) → open it from the home-screen icon. | The icon is the **green** FitLife icon; the app opens full screen with no browser bar; you stay signed in after closing and reopening it. |
| 5 | **Push notifications** | In the installed app: More → Settings → Notifications → Allow. Close the app. A coach (test-site coach login) sends this member a note. | Within about a minute, a notification arrives with the app closed; tapping it opens FitLife on the right screen. Settings no longer says "not registered". |
